# Workshop: Data Literacy

#### <i>Lantern Peak Supply Co.: Source Notes</i>

<p style="border-bottom: 1px solid lightgrey;"></p>

These are the five places Lantern Peak's leadership team has been getting its numbers. Everything here is fictional. You'll use these notes in Module 02 to build a source log.

**Source A: Finance monthly revenue report** (`raw/finance_monthly_revenue.csv`)
Produced by the Finance team after each month closes and reviewed by the controller. It counts revenue in the month an order ships, and subtracts refunds in the month they are paid. It covers January 2023 through December 2025.

**Source B: Order system export** (`raw/orders_raw.csv`, `raw/order_items_raw.csv`, `raw/returns_raw.csv`)
Exported from the online store platform by the digital team. It holds every order as placed, including cancelled ones. The 2023 records were migrated from the previous store system. The export file was loaded a second time in early 2026 after the first load timed out. The store team places test orders to check the checkout flow.

**Source C: Summit Social Ads one-pager** (a vendor handout, fictional vendor)
"Brands that acquire customers through paid social earn 3x return on ad spend, and those customers stay for years." A footnote says the figures are based on "selected client results." The handout gives no number of clients, no time period, and no definition of "return." Summit Social Ads sells paid social advertising.

**Source D: Northern Outdoor Retail Alliance press release** (a trade group, fictional)
"Online outdoor gear sales grew 12 percent in 2025." The release says the figure comes from a survey of the group's 40 member retailers, and that 31 of them replied. Sales were self-reported, and the release does not say how "online sales" was defined. A closing line says the report was made possible with support from several sponsors, including Summit Social Ads. Most members are long-established stores with a large physical presence.

**Source E: Customer satisfaction survey** (internal marketing team)
An email survey sent after purchases from July through December 2025. Replies were voluntary and no incentive was offered. The marketing team's summary reads: "94 percent of customers are satisfied." The reply data is in `supplements/survey_responses.csv`.
