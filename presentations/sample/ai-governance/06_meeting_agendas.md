# Meeting agendas and sample decisions

**Adventure Works Cycles** | *Example document*

Two recurring meetings replace the committee tree. An agenda with no decisions on it is a status meeting, so every Decision Board agenda lists the decisions up front.

| Time | Item | Lead | Outcome sought |

| 0:00 to 0:10 | Scorecard: Coverage 100%, Trust 90%, Use 100% (run 3) | Data Lead | Note the trend |
| 0:10 to 0:35 | Decisions (below) | Chair | Four decisions made |
| 0:35 to 0:45 | Risks and escalations | Chair | Owners assigned |

## How a decision is recorded

Every decision gets a reference, a one-line statement, who recommended it, and the outcome. The log is a simple list, kept with the charter, and reviewed at the 12-month charter review.
