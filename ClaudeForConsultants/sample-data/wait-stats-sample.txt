Additional detail the DBA included:
- Top PAGELATCH_EX waits, when correlated to page IDs in a follow-up trace, mostly pointed at
  (a) the last page of the clustered index on dbo.Orders, and
  (b) pages inside tempdb (PFS/GAM/SGAM-range page IDs).
- tempdb has 1 data file. tempdb sizing had been increased after a prior incident but autogrow
  events still occurred throughout this window (see error log excerpt).
- LCK_M_X waits correlate in time with the "Lock request time out" messages in the error log,
  concentrated on dbo.Orders / dbo.OrderStatus during checkout writes.
- The checkout stored procedure (`dbo.usp_CompleteCheckout`) creates two local temp tables
  (`#cartItems`, `#priceCalc`) on every single execution and this procedure runs on every checkout.
- dbo.Orders uses an ever-increasing IDENTITY column as its clustering key.
